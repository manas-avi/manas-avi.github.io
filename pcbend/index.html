Arrives flat. Folds into light.

  1. 1 Arrives flat

    One thin circuit board, with the LEDs already on it.

  2. 2 Fold it by hand

    Hinges cut into the board let it bend exactly where it should. About 10 minutes for a small shape, up to 2 hours for a big one.

  3. 3 Plug in and play

    Connect it over USB-C or Bluetooth, depending on the version, and pick a pattern.

Questions.

When will it ship?

We'll share a clear timeline with early supporters before anyone pays.

How hard is the folding?

About as hard as building a paper model. It takes around 10 minutes for a small shape and up to 2 hours for a big one. Hinges show you where to bend.

Do I need an app?

No download needed. You control it from a browser on your phone or computer.

How does it connect?

Over USB-C or Bluetooth, depending on the version. Wi-Fi and Home Assistant are next on the list, and early supporters help decide the order.

Can I get my own shape?

Yes, through Your Shape. Places are limited at launch.

Is it safe?

It runs on low-voltage power, and the electronics will be certified for the EU market before shipping.
